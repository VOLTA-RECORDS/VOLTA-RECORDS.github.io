The Label

Built on the current, not the noise.

Volta Records was founded to give Afrobeats and rap artists a home where the work speaks first — no shortcuts, no manufactured sound. The name comes from voltage: the charge behind a great record, the current that moves a room before a single word is understood.

We work close to the ground — from the studio session to the release plan — helping artists shape a sound that's honest to them and built to travel: from the streets of Nigeria to global playlists.

Every artist on Volta keeps ownership of their story. Our job is to sharpen it, produce it right, and get it in front of the people who need to hear it.
